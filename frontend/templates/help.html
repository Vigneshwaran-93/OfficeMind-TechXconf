If you are outside India, please search for your local crisis or emergency helpline. We can't guarantee how any third-party service handles confidentiality.

When to involve someone else

If something you're experiencing involves harassment, bullying, discrimination, or unsafe conditions, OfficeMind can help you think it through — but documenting what happened, speaking with HR, a trusted person, or a qualified professional may also be appropriate. This isn't a reflection of how you communicate; some situations need more than conversation.

Your privacy

  • OfficeMind is never shared with your employer.
  • Names, companies, ticket IDs, amounts, and similar details are automatically masked before anything reaches the AI.
  • Raw transcripts are not stored. Only a plan you choose to save is kept, and only in masked form.
  • You can delete your account and all data at any time from your Profile.
